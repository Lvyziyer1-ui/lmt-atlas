60 identity-only entriesCanonical entries without a public MTS governance record
280 condition-response profiles208 constructs · 66 sources · 1,679 measured points

A condition-response profile is a descriptive construct × source × fixed conditions series within the measured concentration range; it is not an enzyme, protocol, independent study, or ranking unit. See the denominator details.
