Curation Policy

curation policy: We do not change frozen release tables from website feedback alone. Feedback creates a review candidate for a future curated data release.

Any new or corrected evidence must remain traceable to literature or patent sources, with explicit methanol concentration, exposure time or addition mode, readout, assay context, and source location where available.

Original residual-activity reports keep their own experimental conditions. Cross-study comparison uses the unified methanol-tolerance score with uncertainty ranges and confidence checks.
