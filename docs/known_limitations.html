Known Limitations

Known Limitations

Use these constraints when interpreting methanol-tolerance scores, evidence tables, and raw residual activity values.

  • Measurement rows are individual data points, not independent samples. The release contains 2857 measurement rows, but the construct-level modeling layer is 611 construct units from 184 sources.
  • Most construct-level records have uncertain confidence. Among the 611 construct units, many entries remain low-support because source evidence is sparse, heterogeneous, or sensitivity-dependent.
  • Incubation temperature is missing from 28.96% of raw direct rows. Missing temperatures are disclosed as missing and are not replaced with room temperature, optimum temperature, or model estimates.
  • Residual activity values should not be compared directly across unmatched temperatures, methods, methanol concentrations, or exposure times. Filter to comparable assay conditions before interpreting raw activity differences.
  • Cross-lab disagreement is real, not a curation error. For nominally matched assays, same enzyme, same condition, different labs can differ by up to 89.0 percentage points in the current matched-source check (LMT_0248 at 50% methanol, 2 h, 25 °C across 2 sources).
  • Low confidence does not mean the enzyme is not methanol tolerant. Sparse or absent display support only says the evidence base is small or uneven, not that the enzyme is not methanol tolerant.

Strict comparison boundary

Strict comparability is limited to released measurements that satisfy the strict condition contract and exact measurement context. Heterogeneous observations remain available with explicit warning and manual_review states.
