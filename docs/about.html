About LMT-Atlas

LMT-Atlas is a curated, methanol-tolerance-specific evidence database for TAG lipases, designed to keep enzyme identity, construct boundaries, assay protocols, measurements, structure display, and provenance auditable.

The database focuses on intrinsic methanol-tolerance evidence and does not treat reactor-scale biodiesel process performance as the primary tolerance label. Documentation and the data dictionary describe the evidence tiers and source-count scopes.

Because assay conditions are heterogeneous, users should filter by incubation temperature, assay method/substrate, methanol concentration, and exposure time before comparing entries. Cross-condition raw residual activity values are not directly comparable.

This release exposes 611 construct-level records with frozen tolerance scores.

Search exports support condition-filtered comparable subsets plus optional tiered labels in conservative mode; exported classification labels are temporary training views derived from frozen scores, not replacement official labels.

The public display tier is a reader summary of evidence support: Evidence-rich support 126, Moderate evidence support 104, Sparse evidence support 201, Evidence absent 180. Low support means sparse evidence, not low methanol tolerance.
